Antonio De Leon

Bayesian Statistics | Machine Learning | Statistical Software

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at the University of California, Santa Cruz. I work with Dr. Bruno Sansó and Dr. Raquel Prado. I develop Bayesian forecasting methods that quantify uncertainty in climate and environmental data. My work focuses on correcting forecast products, combining quantile forecasts into predictive distributions, and implementing the methods in reproducible R/Python software.

Current projects include Bayesian forecast correction and posterior synthesis, quantile readouts for fixed Deep Echo State Network features, and generalized-Bayes methods for fixed-content intervals. I also co-develop and maintain exdqlm, a CRAN package for dynamic and static Bayesian quantile models.
