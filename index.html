Antonio De Leon

Bayesian Statistics | Machine Learning | Statistical Software

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at the University of California, Santa Cruz. I work with Dr. Bruno Sansó and Dr. Raquel Prado. I develop Bayesian methods for probabilistic forecasting and quantile modeling, with applications to hydrologic time series.

My current research includes Bayesian correction and synthesis of hydrologic forecast products, quantile readouts for fixed Deep Echo State Network features, and direct estimation of fixed-content intervals. I also co-develop and maintain exdqlm, a CRAN package for dynamic and static Bayesian quantile models.
